Learn about the Canadian Armed Forces

CAF Guide is an unofficial study guide: public reports, policies, doctrine and history about the CAF, turned into flashcards that come back just before you would forget them.

  • Learning modules you work through one by one
  • Answer in your head, then reveal the answer
  • Track cards learned, modules completed and your streak
Made by a civilian, for learning. This is a personal study project by someone interested in the field. It is not an official CAF or DND website and is not endorsed by them.
